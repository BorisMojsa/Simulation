Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

What this app is

Football Career Simulator is a browser-based career simulation that runs locally on your device. It does not require an account and does not connect to a backend service for gameplay.

Data we collect

We do not collect personal information through this site. Player names, nationality, and career choices you enter stay in your browser session for the current game and are not uploaded to a server we control.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or third-party marketing pixels. Your browser may still keep ordinary technical cache files for pages and images you load.

Local storage

The current version does not intentionally write career saves to persistent storage. If save features are added later, this policy will be updated to describe what is stored and how to clear it.

Third-party content

Club badges and related assets are loaded from this project’s own files. Fonts may be loaded from Google Fonts when you open the site online; that request is handled by Google under their own terms.

Children

The simulator is a general entertainment product. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions about use by a child, contact the address below.

Contact

For privacy questions about this project, email privacy@footballcareersim.local.
