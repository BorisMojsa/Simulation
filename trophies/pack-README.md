# Static transparent football trophies

49 trophy assets: 46 photographic PNG cutouts, one black PNG silhouette, and two SVG files. No GIFs, APNG animations, or animated SVGs.

- leagues/: 21 assets
- cups/: 18 assets
- international/: 10 assets

Most photographic PNGs are 300 × 300 pixels, matching their downloaded source resolution. Categoría Primera A is 288 × 636; the Supporters’ Shield is 1536 × 1024; the silhouettes retain the source/rendered resolution. SVGs can scale.

Requested special formats: Categoría Primera A uses a real transparent trophy photo; U.S. Open Cup is a black silhouette; Taça de Portugal and Norwegian Cup (Kongepokal) are SVGs. Nations League uses the UEFA men’s trophy.

Ligue 2 uses the silver trophy first awarded in May 2025. Liga Profesional uses the league tournament trophy from the 2025.07 collection. The Supporters’ Shield was made from a real photo with AI background extraction; some fine engraved lettering may differ.

## Special asset credits

- **Categoría Primera A:** Original transparent trophy photograph from the sortitoutsi Trophy Pack forum. Downloaded unchanged at 288 × 636 pixels; no AI image generation or restyling. Source: https://sortitoutsi.net/content/55584/logos-request-thread-2?page=14
- **U.S. Open Cup:** Trophy outline extracted from the crest as native SVG paths, shield border and lettering removed, base closed, and rendered to transparent PNG. Original crest belongs to U.S. Soccer. Official competition page: https://www.ussoccer.com/us-open-cup
- **Taça de Portugal:** Original SVG unchanged. Author: VitorEmanuelCarvalhoPeixoto. License: CC BY-SA 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
- **Norwegian Cup:** Original Harald V Kongepokal SVG unchanged. Author: Realismadder. License: CC BY-SA 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/. Includes embedded raster details inside the SVG.
- **Supporters’ Shield:** Made using AI background extraction from a real trophy photograph; stadium and display stand removed. Some fine engraved lettering may differ from the source photo.
